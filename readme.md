## 📋 **Overview**

Straw Hat Ledger is a feature-rich task management application themed after the popular anime **One Piece**. It helps you track your daily tasks, focus time, and work hours with a pirate-inspired interface.

### ✨ **Features**

- ✅ **Task Management** - Add, complete, and delete tasks
- 🎯 **Priority Levels** - High (🔥), Medium (⚖️), Low (🍃)
- 📅 **Due Dates** - Set and track task deadlines
- ⚠️ **Overdue Alerts** - Visual warnings for overdue tasks
- 📊 **Statistics** - Track open tasks, completed today, overdue, and completion rate
- ⏱️ **Focus Timer** - Pomodoro-style timer with presets (5, 15, 25, 45, 60 min)
- 🕐 **Work Hours Tracker** - Track total working time per day
- 📜 **Task History** - View when tasks were added, completed, and time taken
- 🏴‍☠️ **One Piece Theme** - Straw hat colors, pirate aesthetics, waving flag
- 💾 **Local Storage** - All data saved locally in your browser
- 📱 **Responsive Design** - Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile
